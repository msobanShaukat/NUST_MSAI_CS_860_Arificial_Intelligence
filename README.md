## 📌 Course Overview

> *"What do web search, speech recognition, face recognition, machine translation,
> autonomous driving, and automatic scheduling have in common? These are all complex
> real-world problems — and the goal of AI is to tackle them with rigorous mathematical tools."*

This course introduces the **foundational principles** driving modern AI applications. Students explore problem formulation, search strategies, game theory, Markov decision processes, probabilistic reasoning, machine learning, deep learning, and the application of AI in robotics. It is a **core postgraduate course** designed to build both theoretical depth and hands-on practical skills in Artificial Intelligence.

## 🎯 Course Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- ✅ **Understand** the techniques and theory developed in major areas of AI and the current state of the art
- ✅ **Identify** the type of an AI problem and formally formulate it
- ✅ **Select** a suitable tool or algorithm for any given formulated AI problem
- ✅ **Implement and Deploy** AI solutions to real-world complex problems
